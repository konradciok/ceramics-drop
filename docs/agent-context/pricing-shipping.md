# Pricing & Shipping

### Pricing & Shipping

Multi-currency, defined in `src/lib/pricing.ts`. `PRICE_PLN`, `PRICE_EUR`, and `PRICE_GBP` give per-category prices (whole units); `priceOfCurrency(product, currency)` returns the display price in a given currency. The display currency comes from the `currency_pref` cookie (`src/lib/currency.ts` / server reader `getCurrency` in `currency.server.ts`), not the locale: `pl → PLN`; other locales default to EUR and can switch to GBP, seeded from `CF-IPCountry` and clamped to a switchable currency. Conversion to minor units happens at checkout via `toGrosze()` (PLN×100) / `toEuroCents()` (EUR×100) / `toGBPPence()` (GBP×100). Shipping: the **locker price is per zone** — parcels leave from mainland Spain, zone `es` (Spain) **43 zł / 10 € / 9 £**, zone `eu` (every other destination, Poland included) **65 zł / 15 € / 13 £** (`DEFAULT_LOCKER_SHIPPING`, CMS resource `lockers`); a destination's zone is `DESTINATIONS[country].zone` (`src/lib/delivery/destinations.ts`) and `deliveryRatesFor()` (`src/lib/shipping-rates/delivery-rates.ts`) composes the per-method table every surface and checkout use. Kurier **30 zł / 10 € / 12 £** and Odbiór osobisty **0** stay in the `domestic` table (`SHIPPING_PLN` / `SHIPPING_EUR` / `SHIPPING_GBP`, whose `paczkomat` column is no longer read). **Courier delivery is switched off** (`COURIER_ENABLED = false`, since 2026-11-24 — the stock ships from Spain and InPost España reaches Poland only at lockers/points): checkout, PDP and /dostawa-i-zwroty offer the locker only and `validateDelivery()` refuses `kurier`. **Automatic ShipX shipments are switched off too** (`SHIPX_AUTOMATIC_SHIPMENTS`, `src/lib/delivery/destinations.ts`). **Studio pickup is switched off** (`STUDIO_PICKUP_ENABLED = false` in `pricing.ts`, since 2026-11-24): customers can pick only `CHECKOUT_DELIVERY_METHODS` (paczkomat, kurier), `validateDelivery()` refuses `odbior` (`invalid_method`), and the cart, PDP delivery block, /dostawa-i-zwroty, homepage and contact page stop offering it. `odbior` stays a `DeliveryMethod` for historical orders, the admin and the gift-card checkout's no-delivery sentinel; flip the flag to bring pickup back. Delivery details are validated in `src/lib/delivery/validate.ts` (`validateDelivery()`); carrier adapters live in `src/lib/carriers/` (ShipX in `carriers/inpost-shipx/`, driven by the carrier-agnostic `createOrderShipment` in `src/lib/shipment.ts`). The cart's delivery country select lists `checkoutCountries()` (preselected by `defaultCheckoutCountry`: CF-IPCountry, else locale) and switches the price to that country's zone. Poland picks a Paczkomat in the InPost Geowidget (v5 custom element, `src/components/shop/GeowidgetPicker.tsx`); Sendcloud countries pick an InPost España locker / Punto Pack point in Sendcloud's hosted picker (`src/components/shop/SendcloudPointPicker.tsx`, `embed.sendcloud.sc` in the CSP), which checkout re-checks server-side. **Shipping rates are CMS-published** (`shipping_rates`, resources `lockers` (locker price by zone) and `domestic` (courier/pickup), served by the CMS API; the print-only `international` resource is dormant since the print shop left on 2026-11-22 — still in the CMS contract, displayed and charged nowhere): checkout charges from the published table and **every surface that displays a shipping price** (cart, ceramic PDP, JSON-LD `shippingDetails`, the /dostawa-i-zwroty copy via `{locker}`/`{courier}`) reads the same table through `getShippingRatesForDisplay()` (`src/lib/shipping-rates/get.ts`) — never the `SHIPPING_*` code constants, which are only the outage fallback (`display-parity.test.ts` enforces it). Publishing runbook: `docs/shipping-rates-runbook.md`. (The merchant feeds are retired, so no Merchant Center re-fetch follows a publish.)
