### Pricing & Shipping

Multi-currency, defined in `src/lib/pricing.ts`. `PRICE_PLN`, `PRICE_EUR`, and `PRICE_GBP` give per-category prices (whole units); `priceOfCurrency(product, currency)` returns the display price in a given currency. The display currency comes from the `currency_pref` cookie (`src/lib/currency.ts` / server reader `getCurrency` in `currency.server.ts`), not the locale: `pl → PLN`; other locales default to EUR and can switch to GBP, seeded from `CF-IPCountry` and clamped to a switchable currency. Conversion to minor units happens at checkout via `toGrosze()` (PLN×100) / `toEuroCents()` (EUR×100) / `toGBPPence()` (GBP×100). Shipping: Paczkomat **20 zł / 5 € / 5 £**, Kurier **30 zł / 10 € / 12 £**, Odbiór osobisty (Tenerife studio) **0** (`SHIPPING_PLN` / `SHIPPING_EUR` / `SHIPPING_GBP`). **Courier delivery is switched off** (`COURIER_ENABLED = false`, since 2026-11-24 — the stock ships from Spain and InPost España reaches Poland only at lockers/points): checkout, PDP and /dostawa-i-zwroty offer the locker only and `validateDelivery()` refuses `kurier`. **Automatic ShipX shipments are switched off too** (`SHIPX_AUTOMATIC_SHIPMENTS`, `src/lib/delivery/destinations.ts`). **Studio pickup is switched off** (`STUDIO_PICKUP_ENABLED = false` in `pricing.ts`, since 2026-11-24): customers can pick only `CHECKOUT_DELIVERY_METHODS` (paczkomat, kurier), `validateDelivery()` refuses `odbior` (`invalid_method`), and the cart, PDP delivery block, /dostawa-i-zwroty, homepage and contact page stop offering it. `odbior` stays a `DeliveryMethod` for historical orders, the admin and the gift-card checkout's no-delivery sentinel; flip the flag to bring pickup back. Delivery details are validated in `src/lib/delivery/validate.ts` (`validateDelivery()`); carrier adapters live in `src/lib/carriers/` (ShipX in `carriers/inpost-shipx/`, driven by the carrier-agnostic `createOrderShipment` in `src/lib/shipment.ts`). InPost Geowidget (v5 custom element) is rendered in `src/components/shop/GeowidgetPicker.tsx` for locker selection. **Shipping rates are CMS-published** (`shipping_rates`, resource `domestic`, served by the CMS API; the print-only `international` resource is dormant since the print shop left on 2026-11-22 — still in the CMS contract, displayed and charged nowhere): checkout charges from the published table and **every surface that displays a shipping price** (cart, ceramic PDP, JSON-LD `shippingDetails`, the /dostawa-i-zwroty copy via `{locker}`/`{courier}`) reads the same table through `getShippingRatesForDisplay()` (`src/lib/shipping-rates/get.ts`) — never the `SHIPPING_*` code constants, which are only the outage fallback (`display-parity.test.ts` enforces it). Publishing runbook: `docs/shipping-rates-runbook.md`. (The merchant feeds are retired, so no Merchant Center re-fetch follows a publish.)
