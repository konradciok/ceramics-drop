Frames
Frame your artwork with our premium quality frames, made of sustainably grown FSC-certified wood. Whether you opt for the timeless beauty of solid oak or the vibrant hues of our painted wood frames, each frame is manufactured and assembled in Denmark.Equipped with two metal hangers on the back, our frames offer flexibility, allowing you to hang them both vertically and horizontally on your wall.

Production & Sustainability
Each art print is made to order, helping to reduce paper waste. Produced as museum-grade giclée prints on 245 g FSC™-certified archival-quality art paper from responsibly sourced materials, each print features a matte, natural finish and a lightly textured surface, designed to preserve colour depth and fine detail over time. 

Giclée is a printing technique used to attain intense, saturated colours as well as a greater variety of depth and nuance. Combined with the premium art paper, it guarantees the archival conservation of colours for the highest possible age resistance. 

Care & Maintenance 
To preserve the quality and appearance of your art print, avoid direct sunlight, excessive humidity, and contact with moisture. We recommend framing the print behind glass or acrylic for optimal protection. Handle with clean, dry hands and avoid touching the printed surface directly.
